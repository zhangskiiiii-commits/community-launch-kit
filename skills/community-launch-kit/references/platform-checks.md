# Platform checks

Rules change. Re-read the community and destination on each run. These links are
starting points, not stored permission to promote.

## Reddit

- Open the specific post, relevant comments, community rules and pinned posts.
- If promotion is restricted to a weekly thread, find that current thread and
  check its conditions separately. A useful reply can still be self-promotion.
- Record visible restrictions on links, images, flair, format and account eligibility.
  “No rule found” means unknown, not allowed.
- Check removed/locked/archived state; do not infer it from age alone.
- Relevance cannot override a prohibition. Do not duplicate promotional replies
  across communities or move to unsolicited DMs after deletion.
- No universal “90% helpful / 10% promotion” rule grants permission across Reddit.
- A deletion is not automatically evidence of a shadowban. Filters, account
  eligibility and moderator decisions need separate evidence.

Official starting points, reviewed 2026-11-12:
- [Spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)
- [Why can't I see my post?](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360045989712-Why-can-t-I-see-my-post)
- [Post actions and locking](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/15484642147988-Post-Actions-Lock-NSFW-and-Spoiler)

## Facebook

- Read the specific post, group About, rules and announcements. A group description
  alone is not proof of a member's need.
- Public visibility, membership and permission to post are separate checks. Admin
  approval may still apply. Record missing checks explicitly.
- Use public access or a user-authorized browser session. Do not request cookies,
  scrape private groups, bypass login or join groups without instruction.
- Login walls and snippets are discovery leads, not verified needs. Provide direct
  links and explain which manual checks remain.
- A group changing to public does not imply older private discussions became public.
- Verify images/links in the exact surface. Avoid invented composer links; a
  verified group URL plus clear navigation is sufficient.

Official background, reviewed 2026-11-12:
- [Public groups and admin controls](https://about.fb.com/news/2020/10/privacy-matters-the-new-public-groups/)
- [Group privacy changes and existing content](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/11/facebook-update-group-admins-flexibility-protecting-member-privacy/)

## Search notes

Replace the angle-bracket terms in these query shapes:

```text
site:reddit.com/r/ "how do you" "<problem>"
site:reddit.com/r/ "any app" "<task>"
site:reddit.com/r/ "looking for" "<workflow>"
site:facebook.com/groups/ "<problem>" "recommend"
"<community name>" "self promotion"
"<community name>" "weekly promotion thread"
```

Snippets, crawl dates and popularity cannot establish current demand or permission.
Paraphrase only what was read. Recheck promising destinations before any later
user-authorized publishing action.
