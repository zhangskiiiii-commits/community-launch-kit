# Copy and visuals

## A draft someone can use

Use the audience's language and the question's context. Usually:
1. Address one detail of the question or offer a practical first step.
2. Explain a matching feature supported by a confirmed fact ID.
3. Include a plain maker disclosure in the body, such as “I’m building this.”
4. Suggest one modest next step allowed by the destination.

Do not paste the same generic body into ten communities. Explain each draft's fit.
Do not add “free”, “available now”, outcome numbers, professional endorsements or
capabilities without verified facts.

New posts need descriptive titles and complete bodies. Replies should make sense
under the actual original question. Promotion threads may impose formats and
length limits. In prohibited venues, keep drafts on hold or offer a non-promotional
answer without product mentions, links or solicitation.

## Choose visuals by purpose

| Purpose | Asset | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show a feature | Approved screenshot with concise annotation | Accurate UI and claim |
| Explain a workflow | Labelled illustration or 2–3 step diagram | Identify as illustration if it could look like real UI |
| Ask for design feedback | Authorized screenshot or marked prototype | Do not imply planned features shipped |
| Reply where images are unsuitable | Text only | Explain why; do not force attachments |

Record destination, purpose, source assets, exact on-image text, language, aspect
ratio, composition, alt text and caption. Use the host's image tool to produce
actual files, following its editing/reference-image instructions. Inspect source
assets first. Never substitute prompts or placeholder files for completed images.

Brief format:

```text
Opportunity: R01
Purpose: explain the single feature relevant to this post
Source: approved screenshot, or “original conceptual illustration”
Layout: one focal element, readable labels, little text
Language and exact text: ...
Aspect ratio: choose for this destination; do not assume a fixed platform rule
Accuracy: facts F1 and F2 only; no invented UI or results
Alt text: ...
Caption: ...
```

Map assets to opportunity IDs. Reuse an approved base asset when relevant, but
do not count reused files as distinct images. Inspect legibility, accuracy, privacy
and cropping after generation. Record a relative path only after saving succeeds.
